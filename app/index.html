    Road to the platinum

    Work the steps in order. Nothing here is missable except the two trophies flagged in step 2, so the cleanup phase can be done in any order after the credits.

    1. Step 1 — Play the story. Follow Lucia and Jason from the prologue to the final score. Story trophies pop as you go, and the map opens up as you progress. No difficulty requirement.
    2. Step 2 — Mind the two missable trophies. Before the late-story mission with the informant, make a manual save (Loose Ends — spare them). At the final heist planning board, pick Lucia's plan (The Hard Call). Both are flagged in the checklist.
    3. Step 3 — Clear the heists and stranger missions. Finish every major heist and every stranger mission. These feed Kingpin's Vault, Friends in Low Places and the completion counter.
    4. Step 4 — Explore and collect. Visit every location, then sweep the collectible sets — hidden packages, postcards, vinyl records and buried treasure — district by district with the checklist open.
    5. Step 5 — Activities and races. Win each race series, finish the stunt jumps, and work through the clubs, golf, pool, darts, fishing, hunting and gym activities.
    6. Step 6 — Cleanup and grind. Mop up the cumulative trophies — headshots, miles driven, money spent — then check the stats page for 100 percent. The platinum pops with the last trophy.
